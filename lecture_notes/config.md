**notes_folder**: the folder of lecture PDFs to search, including its subfolders. Keep it to lecture notes only:
exercise sheets and past papers there will appear in the results too. `~` means your home folder. Leave it empty
and the add-on asks for a folder the first time it needs one; you can also change it with
Tools > Lecture Notes: Choose notes folder.

**shortcut**: the key that searches your notes for the card under review. It only works while reviewing.
